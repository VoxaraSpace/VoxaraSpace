# Reporting a security problem

Write to **security@voxaraspace.com**. Include what you found, how to
reproduce it, and which build (Settings, About) you were running. Please do
not open a public issue for anything that could be used against people
before it is fixed.

What to expect: an acknowledgement within two days, a fix for confirmed
problems as fast as we can ship one (the app self-updates, so fixes reach
everyone quickly), and credit in the release notes if you want it.

In scope: the desktop app, the bot SDK, the WebSocket protocol as used by the
app, and the voxaraspace.com service. Please test against your own account
and your own spaces only; do not read, modify or disrupt other people's data.

Out of scope: denial of service, reports that need physical access to an
unlocked computer, and social engineering of Voxara staff or users.
